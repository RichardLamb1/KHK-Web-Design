About Us

Kappa Eta Kappa is a co-ed professional fraternity dedicated to promoting excellence in engineering and computer science. Since our founding, we have been committed to developing future leaders through professional growth and meaningful relationships.

While our membership is primarily composed of Engineering, Computer Science, and Data Science majors, we welcome students from all disciplines who share our commitment to professional excellence and community.

Our Delta Chapter at UW-Madison is proud to serve the campus community through professional development, social engagement, and lifelong friendships.

Our Vision

Building the Engineers who will lead the world of tomorrow.

Our Mission

We create an environment that fosters technical excellence, leadership skills, and lifelong networks to inspire our members to be their very best.

Our Values

The principles that guide who we are and how we grow together.

Lifelong Community

The bonds between our members are the most important part of our organization. Their long-lasting friendships make it such that they never feel alone and no goal feels out of reach. Creating this sense of belonging is not a consequence of being part of KHK, but rather one of its core tenets.

Pursuing Excellence

To change the world, one must sharpen their mind and dedicate themselves to their craft. Our members seek to enhance their capabilities to the utmost, challenging each other to continuously improve.

Foresight

The future is subject to not only our own actions, but also to the actions of those who come after. As both individual people and those part of the KHK community, we think not only of the past and present, but of the future and all that comes with it, and seek to mentor our juniors who will take up the mantle from us.

Cultivating Leadership

Leadership is not just a title. It is taking initiative, being unafraid of challenge, serving others, and rallying together people for a cause greater than themselves. KHK believes in empowering all of its members to be inspiring leaders.

Service as our Greatest Call

The highest call for any human is to improve the world around them. Strengthened by our bonds, tempered by our practice, prepared by our foresight, and experiencing the pressures of leadership, we seek to make this world a better place than when we first came into it.
