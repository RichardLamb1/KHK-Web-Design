To change your password, please enter your current password and the new password you would like to use. After submitting, the system will log you out and you will need to log in with your new password. To keep KHK secure, your password must meet the following requirements:

  • At least 8 characters long
  • Contains at least one uppercase letter
  • Contains at least one lowercase letter
  • Contains at least one number
  • Contains at least one special character (e.g., !@#$%^&*)
  • Does not contain any dictionary words, names, your username, email addresses, phone numbers, or other easily guessable information

Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) for added security. You will need to use an authenticator app to generate a code each time you log in.

As an [administrator | executive board member], you're required to have two-factor authentication enabled for your account. If you disable it, you will lose access to administrative functions until it is re-enabled.
