Voting

To vote in this election, please fill out the ballot below. For ranked choice elections, drag and drop the candidates in order of preference. For simple choice elections, follow the directions provided.

Once you have completed your ballot, click the "Lock & Submit" button below it. The system will then validate your selections and submit your ballot. You will then be able to see the real-time results.

Only you can see who you have voted for. Once you submit your ballot, the system will keep your vote anonymous. The only information associated with your identity will be a flag indicating that you voted in this election.
