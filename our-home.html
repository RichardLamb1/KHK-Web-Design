Fraternity House

The chapter house at 114 N Orchard St, Madison, WI 53715 is the center of our day-to-day chapter life, giving members a place to live, study, build projects, and spend time together.

From the front porch to the basement workshop spaces, the house is designed to feel like a second home. It is close enough to campus to make the walk simple, but still has the space and atmosphere that make it unique.

House History

A home with a long line of chapter houses behind it

The chapter has moved a few times over the years, and each house added something to the story.

1920s

The first chapter house

The earliest house was a modest converted home near campus, where the chapter learned how to keep a house running and turned a small front room into a meeting space that quickly became the center of chapter life.

1950s

A bigger place for a growing chapter

As the chapter expanded, we moved into a larger home with more bedrooms and better common areas. That house hosted countless dinners, study sessions, and late-night conversations that became part of chapter tradition.

Today

The current house at Orchard Street

Our current house gives members a place that is close to campus and tailored to the way the chapter works today, with room for living, making things, hosting events, and keeping the chapter connected.

Directions

Easy to find from campus

The chapter house is located at 114 N Orchard St Madison WI 53715. It is only one block away from Union South, two blocks away from Engineering Hall, and three blocks away from Morgridge Hall.

  • Walk north from Union South and you are there in a minute.
  • Use Engineering Hall as a simple mid-point when giving directions to visitors.
  • Morgridge Hall is just a short, direct walk from the front door.

Member Housing

Rooms at the house are reserved exclusively for members, and every room is a solo room.
